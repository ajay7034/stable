A clearer enquiry makes a better starting point.

What to include when you send a quotation request for construction or finishing materials.

Make the request easy to understand

Organise your list by material category and describe each item clearly. Include dimensions, quantities, units and any approved product requirements.

Separate confirmed details from questions

Flag uncertain items rather than guessing. This helps the conversation focus on the information that needs to be checked before a quotation can be confirmed.

Share your timing

Include your required date and project location. Availability and any fulfilment arrangements must be discussed and confirmed by the team.

Keep the conversation open

You can submit a material list or PDF BOQ through the quotation form. Final pricing and availability are determined by the team.
