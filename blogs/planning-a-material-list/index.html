Before you build, start with a better material list.

A few practical details can make your next building materials enquiry clearer and more useful.

Start with the work, not just the product

Describe the task, the location and the application. A material name alone may not communicate the dimensions, finish or installation requirements your project needs.

Write down quantities and units

Keep lengths, areas, pieces and pack sizes separate. Ask your project professional to confirm allowances and suitability rather than assuming two similarly named materials are interchangeable.

Include the details that matter

Add drawings, approved specifications or a bill of quantities where available. Mark any item that needs clarification and make your required date clear.

Ask before confirming

Pricing, availability and exact specifications should be confirmed by the supplier before you proceed. Stable welcomes material enquiries for wholesale and retail requirements in Ajman.
